## Start in your workspace

Open your usual work folder in **Claude Code, Codex or another assistant that can read your files**. Paste this:

```text
Read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Valentis-AI-Labs-Ltd/ai-workspace-audit/main/START-HERE.md and start my Valentis AI Workspace Audit.
```

Your assistant will welcome you and wait for **GO**. The audit starts by looking, with no changes to your files. You choose which improvements to make.

| 01 / Review | 02 / Improve | 03 / Make it yours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find what works and what gets in the way. | Improve one real workflow and check the result. | Keep the useful methods, context and handovers. |

It adapts to your experience and explains the choices. Start with your current setup or get help choosing a sensible home for a new one.

## Prefer to download?

1. **[Download](https://github.com/Valentis-AI-Labs-Ltd/ai-workspace-audit/releases/latest/download/Valentis-AI-Workspace-Audit.zip) and unzip.** Open **OPEN-FIRST.html** for the copy button and short instructions.
2. **Keep the kit in its own folder.** You can place that folder inside your workspace without replacing anything.
3. **Ask your assistant:** “Read START-HERE.md in the Valentis AI Workspace Audit kit and begin.” If it cannot find the file, attach it or give its full path.

No GitHub account or terminal commands needed.

<details>
<summary><strong>What’s inside?</strong></summary>

- A guided audit and practical help with the improvements you choose.
- Context maps, shared instructions and handover templates.
- Example skills for briefing, reviewing and resuming work.
- Optional guidance on Claude/Codex portability, backups and a first scheduled routine.

There is no fixed folder system to adopt. Use the pieces that help your work.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I use a regular chat?</strong></summary>

Yes. Attach `START-HERE.md` and ask the assistant to begin. Without folder access, it will guide a smaller review using examples you choose to share. It cannot scan files it cannot access.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What happens to my files?</strong></summary>

The kit contains instructions and examples. It has no reporting service or analytics and does not send your audit to Valentis. Your chosen AI provider handles the material you give it under its own settings and terms. Only share files you are authorised to use, and keep credentials and private customer records out of shared examples.

Use and adapt the kit under the [MIT licence](LICENSE).

</details>
