# Prepare a usable brief

Resolve the workspace, outcome and intended recipient. Read applicable guidance and selected sources. Ask for missing facts that affect the work; do not blend unrelated client material.

Use the existing team format where useful. Include the outcome, relevant evidence, deliverable, constraints, acceptance checks and any known owner/reviewer. Distinguish supported facts from assumptions and decisions still needed. Do not invent assignments or commitments.

Save the brief in the project's existing home and check that another person can use it without the chat transcript. Drafting a brief does not authorise execution, spending or publication.
