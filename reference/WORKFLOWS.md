# Choose one real task

These are ideas, not a checklist. Pick the repeated task that creates the most friction, then follow it from input to a checked output.

| Work | A useful first workflow | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Writing and marketing | Research and a brief → draft → editorial review | Sources, audience, tone, claims and intended action |
| Client delivery | Selected client context → brief → team handover | Correct client, scope, owner, reviewer and deliverable |
| Operations | Supplied records → a short decision brief | Source dates, missing inputs and the actual decision needed |
| Software | A small requirement → change → relevant checks | Existing guidance, behaviour and what was actually tested |
| Research and strategy | A question → evidence → options → recommendation | Source quality, uncertainty and assumptions |
| Creative production | Approved concept → asset brief or generation plan | Exact copy, references, formats and permission for paid actions |

Keep evidence separate from hypotheses. Do not invent customer quotes, business facts or results. Use the team's existing approval standards and current sources for changeable facts.

Give staff a brief they can use without an AI chat. Preserve their responsibilities and do not invent assignments. Keep client-specific information separate while sharing neutral methods.

After one successful manual run, consider a reusable skill or a draft-only routine. If the task still needs a human decision every few minutes, it is probably better as an interactive workflow for now.
